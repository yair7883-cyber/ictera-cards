# Bot 1: Trend Scout (weekly, Sunday)
Goal: see what carousels in the trading niche are running right now, and turn it into ORIGINAL topic ideas for ICTera.
Sources (use what is connected): vidIQ (instagram_tiktok_outlier_search, tiktok_trending_sound_search, ig_profile_reels),
HasData Instagram posts/reels, Metricool competitors if available. Niche: day trading, futures/NQ, ICT, smart money,
prop firms, trading psychology, in Hebrew and English.
Output (write to plan/trends/<yyyy-mm-dd>.md and push to the branch):
1. Top 10 fast-growing carousels/reels: account, hook, format, slides, views/likes vs the account's average, why it works.
2. Patterns: hook types, slide count, topics, posting hours, trending sounds.
3. 3 to 5 ideas for ICTera in our style. "Recreate" means re-interpret the CONCEPT in our own words and design.
   Never copy another account's text, images or structure one to one. Check every idea against BRIEF.md (no certainty,
   no profit promises) and against topics we already covered. Drop anything you cannot verify.
4. Add accepted-looking ideas to the queue in plan/ideas.md. Do not build or publish anything; Yair approves first.
