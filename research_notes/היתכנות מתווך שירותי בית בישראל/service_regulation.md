## 1. Contractor registration (חוק רישום קבלנים לעבודות הנדסה בנאיות, התשכ"ט-1969) for garden development / landscaping, the threshold, the middleman, and penalties

### Takeaway
Pure gardening work (planting, lawn, garden maintenance, drip lines) does not appear in the statutory definition of "עבודות הנדסה בנאיות". Garden hardscape does: paths, sidewalks, fencing and parking fall under sub-branch 111 "פיתוח חצרות" within main branch 100 (בנייה). Such work on one site above the indexed threshold must be done by a registered contractor. Snippets of the Nevo consolidated text give the threshold as 108,500 NIS for a main branch and 56,750 NIS for a sub-branch; this is not verified directly. The statutory definition of "contractor" is whoever "מקבל על עצמו עבודות אלה למען אחר". On that wording, a brand that signs with the customer and subcontracts is very likely the "contractor" for the law's purposes, even if a registered subcontractor does the physical work. That is an inference; I found no ruling directly on point.

**Penalties and enforcement**
- Performing such work in breach of the law is a criminal offence. The maximum is reported as six months' imprisonment and a fine "כנקוב בסעיף 61(א) לחוק העונשין" (from a ruling quoted on a legal site; the penalty-section number and current fine amount are not verified) — [fridmanwork](https://www.fridmanwork.com/MDx-lawyer33653.html)
- A reported sentence for offences "לפי סעיפים 14 ו-16" was a 14,000 NIS fine or 28 days' imprisonment. This is one case, not the statutory maximum, and the exact page is not pinned among the search results — [gsher-law](https://www.gsher-law.com/%D7%90%D7%9B%D7%99%D7%A4%D7%AA_%D7%97%D7%95%D7%A7_%D7%A8%D7%99%D7%A9%D7%95%D7%9D_%D7%A7%D7%91%D7%9C%D7%A0%D7%99%D7%9D); [fridmanwork](https://www.fridmanwork.com/MDx-lawyer33653.html)

### Inferences
- **Mapping of the garden jobs the user named (inference, not a ruling):**
  - Planting, lawn, garden maintenance, drip-irrigation installation inside a private garden, and artificial grass: not in the statutory list (building, roads, bridges, sewage, drainage, ports, water works). Probably no contractor registration is needed, whatever the amount. "Drainage" and "water works" might be argued for large irrigation or drainage jobs; not verified.
  - Paving/cladding in the yard (ריצוף/חיפוי), paths, fences, parking: sub-branch 111 פיתוח חצרות. Registration is needed above about 56,750 NIS per site (sub-branch threshold, if the Nevo figure is current).
  - Retaining walls, built pergolas with foundations, raised decks on foundations: likely "בנייה" (100) or 111. A structural element may require a registered contractor regardless of amount under "מהות מקצועית".
- **Middleman exposure (inference):** the definition turns on who "מקבל על עצמו" the work for another. The party that signs with the homeowner and takes on the job is therefore the "contractor" in the statutory sense, even if a registered subcontractor does the physical work. Subcontracting to a registered contractor protects the subcontractor's leg, not the middleman's. For a hardscape job above the threshold, the safe structure is either (a) the middleman registers in 111/100 (this requires a qualified professional, which an owner without trade expertise does not have — see Gaps), or (b) the registered contractor contracts directly with the customer and the middleman takes a disclosed referral or marketing fee.
- A brand name or marketing copy such as "קבלן פיתוח" or "קבלן גינון רשום" by an unregistered person risks the section 1 title/impersonation offence.
- Keeping each hardscape job under the threshold avoids registration, but splitting one site's work into several contracts to stay under it is likely to be treated as one site's scope ("באתר בניה אחד"). This is an inference; I found no ruling.

- The exact penalty section (section 16?) and current fine amount were not verified. Check the Nevo law text and Penal Law s.61(א).
- Registration requirements were not researched: professional qualification (מהנדס/הנדסאי/experience), fees, processing time, and the bank guarantee or equity required for each classification. Check the Registrar's gov.il pages and the 1988 classification regulations.
- No court ruling or Registrar position was found on a non-performing "broker" that signs with the customer and subcontracts everything. This is a key question for a lawyer specialising in the Contractors Law.
- Whether irrigation-system installation or artificial grass on a prepared base could fall under "עבודות עפר" (210) or "ניקוז" was not determined.

## 2. Water softeners and home water-treatment devices: product approval, standards, salt/brine restrictions, permitted technologies, claims

### Takeaway
Home drinking-water treatment systems must comply with the official (mandatory) Israeli Standard ת"י 1505. The Health Ministry says manufacturing, selling or importing non-compliant home treatment devices is prohibited. I found no official source saying salt-based ion-exchange softeners are banned in private homes. The brine regulations found apply to a "מפעל" with annual thresholds measured in tonnes. Claims of a home ban, including "imprisonment for anyone who installs a salt softener", come from competing sellers of salt-free products and are unverified. Performance and health claims (magnetic or electronic anti-scale) fall under the general misleading-advertising rules of the Consumer Protection Law. No specific regulator ruling on them was found.

### Cited Findings
- The Standards Institution says ת"י 1505 "מערכות לטיפול במי שתייה לשימוש ביתי: סינון וטיהור" is an official standard: "חובה על פי חוק לייבא לייצר ולשווק רק מערכות המותאמות לדרישות התקן". Tests cover filtration and purification efficiency, the product's effect on water quality (e.g. heavy-metal leaching), electrical safety and hydraulic function — [SII: המלצות לרכישה ושימוש במטהרי מים](https://www.sii.org.il/he/watepurifier)
- Health Ministry drinking-water FAQ: "תקן ישראלי מספר 1505 הוכרז כרשמי ולכן חל איסור על יצור, מכירה ויבוא של מכשירי טיפול ביתיים שאינם עומדים בתקן. משרד התמ"ת אחראי על הפיקוח והאכיפה". The ministry has since been renamed; enforcement now sits with the Ministry of Economy's standardization commissioner (inference) — [MoH FAQ](https://www.gov.il/he/pages/drinking_water_faq)
- ת"י 1505 has parts: Part 1 filtration/purification, Part 2 reverse osmosis (Part 2 identical to NSF/ANSI 58) — [SII standard PDF via chamber.org.il](https://www.chamber.org.il/media/147248/1505-1.pdf)
- A separate list exists of official standards that refer to ת"י 5452 (testing of products in contact with drinking water) — [chamber.org.il list](https://www.chamber.org.il/media/166986/%D7%A8%D7%A9%D7%99%D7%9E%D7%AA-%D7%AA%D7%A7%D7%A0%D7%99%D7%9D-%D7%94%D7%9E%D7%A4%D7%A0%D7%99%D7%9D-%D7%9C%D7%AA%D7%A7%D7%9F-5452.pdf). Whether whole-house softeners are covered by 1505 Part 1 or by another standard was **not verified**. A commercial blog says whole-house anti-scale (silicophosphate) systems are tested under Part 1 — [Tipatech](https://www.tipatech.co.il/%D7%94%D7%90%D7%9D-%D7%AA%D7%A7%D7%9F-1505-%D7%91%D7%98%D7%95%D7%97-%D7%9C%D7%A9%D7%AA%D7%99%D7%99%D7%94-2/) (commercial)
- The 2013 drinking-water regulations (תקנות בריאות העם (איכותם התברואית של מי שתייה ומיתקני מי שתייה), התשע"ג-2013, published 26.6.2013) regulate water suppliers' treatment facilities. Under regulation 14 a consumer may ask the supplier to test water at home at the consumer's expense — [Nevo](https://www.nevo.co.il/law_html/law00/125912.htm); [Kolchei Misgav](https://kmisgav.co.il/%D7%90%D7%99%D7%9B%D7%95%D7%AA-%D7%9E%D7%99-%D7%94%D7%A9%D7%AA%D7%99%D7%99%D7%94/). No provision on household softeners was found in the snippets.
- **Brine:** תקנות המים (מניעת זיהום מים) (איסור הזרמת תמלחת למקורות מים), תשנ"ח-1998, quoted in search snippet: "לא יזרים מפעל תמלחת ממפעלו לסביבה או למקור מים, לרבות למערכת ביוב של רשות מקומית, אלא כאמור בתקנת משנה (ב)". "תמלחת" includes ion-exchange regeneration effluent "בכמות של שלוש טונות כלורידים לשנה או שתי טונות נתרן לשנה ומעלה". Rinse water from an ion exchanger may go to sewer if its salt is no more than 5% of the regeneration salt and conductivity stays under a set value — [Nevo](https://www.nevo.co.il/law_html/law01/235_075.htm); [Wikisource](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/%D7%AA%D7%A7%D7%A0%D7%95%D7%AA_%D7%94%D7%9E%D7%99%D7%9D_(%D7%9E%D7%A0%D7%99%D7%A2%D7%AA_%D7%96%D7%99%D7%94%D7%95%D7%9D_%D7%9E%D7%99%D7%9D)_(%D7%90%D7%99%D7%A1%D7%95%D7%A8_%D7%94%D7%96%D7%A8%D7%9E%D7%AA_%D7%AA%D7%9E%D7%9C%D7%97%D7%AA_%D7%9C%D7%9E%D7%A7%D7%95%D7%A8%D7%95%D7%AA_%D7%9E%D7%99%D7%9D)) (the retrieved text was partly garbled; verify)
- **Seller claims of a home ban (contested, commercial):** Tipatech, a seller of alternative systems, headlines "מאסר בפועל לכל מי שמתקין/משתמש במרכך מים על בסיס מלח" — [Tipatech](https://www.tipatech.co.il/%D7%9E%D7%90%D7%A1%D7%A8-%D7%91%D7%A4%D7%95%D7%A2%D7%9C-%D7%9C%D7%9B%D7%9C-%D7%9E%D7%99-%D7%A9%D7%9E%D7%AA%D7%A7%D7%99%D7%9F-%D7%9E%D7%A9%D7%AA%D7%9E%D7%A9-%D7%91%D7%9E%D7%A8%D7%9B%D7%9A-%D7%9E%D7%99/). Markit claims Environmental Protection regulations impose "איסור מוחלט של הזרמת תמלחות ממרככי המלח מעל לצריכה של 5 טון מלח בשנה, ובמתקנים קטנים האיסור חל בכל כמות" — [Markit](https://www.markit.co.il/%D7%A6%D7%9E%D7%A6%D7%95%D7%9D-%D7%94%D7%96%D7%A8%D7%9E%D7%AA-%D7%AA%D7%99%D7%9E%D7%9C%D7%97%D7%AA). Other sellers and forums say home salt softeners are "באופן עקרוני אינו חוקי" but unenforced — [htmag forum](https://htmag.co.il/phpbb/viewtopic.php?t=127193). Meanwhile, mainstream retailers openly sell salt-based home softeners — [WaterStore product page](https://waterstore.co.il/product/%D7%9E%D7%A8%D7%9B%D7%9A-%D7%9E%D7%A7%D7%A1%D7%99-%D7%A7%D7%95%D7%9E%D7%A4%D7%9C%D7%98-%D7%93%D7%99%D7%92%D7%99%D7%98%D7%9C%D7%99-%D7%A2%D7%9D-%D7%9E%D7%9C%D7%97-clack-usa-robosoft/). None of these is an official source.
- **Policy pressure on salinity:** the effluent regulations (תקנות בריאות העם (תקני איכות מי קולחין וכללים לטיהור שפכים), תש"ע-2010) cap chloride in effluent relative to supply water (search summary: 80 mg/L above supply; not verified) — [Nevo](https://www.nevo.co.il/law_html/law01/500_306.htm). A May 2025 letter to the Water Authority says chloride, sodium, boron and fluoride harm the quality of effluent reused for agricultural irrigation — [gov.il letter 13.5.2025](https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/generalpage/water_and_sewerage_corporation_repair3/he/%D7%94%D7%AA%D7%90%D7%97%D7%93%D7%95%D7%AA%20%D7%94%D7%AA%D7%A2%D7%A9%D7%99%D7%99%D7%A0%D7%99%D7%9D%20-%2013.5.2025.pdf). A government report notes household uses raise chloride in wastewater — [gov.il irrigation-with-effluent report](https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/reports/irrigation/ar/wastewater_municipal_wastewater_irrigation-with-treated-wastewater.pdf)
- **Claims:** the Consumer Protection Law, 1981 prohibits misleading consumers by act or omission in any material matter, and the Consumer Protection Authority treats non-disclosure of material product changes as misleading (גילוי דעת 2.24) — [Wikipedia: חוק הגנת הצרכן](https://he.wikipedia.org/wiki/%D7%97%D7%95%D7%A7_%D7%94%D7%92%D7%A0%D7%AA_%D7%94%D7%A6%D7%A8%D7%9B%D7%9F); [ynet](https://www.ynet.co.il/economy/article/sjdj5dru0). Sellers market magnetic and electrolytic devices with claims such as "מונע עד 85%" of scale — [KSDD product page](https://ksdd.co.il/product/%D7%9E%D7%9B%D7%A9%D7%99%D7%A8-%D7%90%D7%9C%D7%A7%D7%98%D7%A8%D7%95%D7%9C%D7%99%D7%98%D7%99-%D7%9E%D7%92%D7%A0%D7%98%D7%99-%D7%A0%D7%92%D7%93-%D7%90%D7%91%D7%A0%D7%99%D7%AA-%D7%A2%D7%9D-%D7%A1%D7%A0/). A commercial article cites Technion research finding magnets ineffective under normal home conditions — [Tamhil](https://tamhil.com/%D7%94%D7%90%D7%9D-%D7%9E%D7%92%D7%A0%D7%98-%D7%99%D7%9B%D7%95%D7%9C-%D7%9C%D7%9E%D7%A0%D7%95%D7%A2-%D7%90%D7%91%D7%A0%D7%99%D7%AA-%D7%91%D7%9E%D7%99%D7%9D/) (secondary; original study not verified).
- Secondary sources note that ion-exchange softeners add sodium to the water, which matters for people with high blood pressure — [Eco-bar guide](https://www.eco-bar.co.il/%D7%90%D7%99%D7%9A-%D7%9C%D7%91%D7%97%D7%95%D7%A8-%D7%9E%D7%A2%D7%A8%D7%9B%D7%AA-%D7%98%D7%99%D7%94%D7%95%D7%A8-%D7%9E%D7%99%D7%9D-%D7%9C%D7%91%D7%99%D7%AA/) (commercial)

### Inferences
- **Technologies on the Israeli market (inference from retail listings above):** salt-based ion-exchange softeners, silicophosphate/anti-scale cartridges, template-assisted crystallisation (TAC) "salt-free" units, and magnetic/electronic devices are all openly sold. No official ban on any of them was found.
- The 1998 brine regulations are framed for a "מפעל" and set annual tonnage thresholds. A single home softener is very likely far below them. Reading these regulations as a ban on household softeners looks like a marketing claim, unless a local water corporation's rules or by-laws say otherwise (not found).
- **Middleman exposure on the product:** the seller of record (the brand) is the one "marketing" the device. Selling a device that does not comply with ת"י 1505, if it applies to the device, exposes the brand, not only the installer. The safe practice is to source only models with an SII 1505 certificate or test report and keep it on file.
- **Claims:** "removes scale", "healthier water", "protects your health" or "certified by the Health Ministry" claims made by the brand are the brand's own representations under the Consumer Protection Law. Magnetic or electronic "softening" claims are the highest-risk category, because these devices do not remove hardness.

### Gaps
- Whether whole-house softeners (as opposed to point-of-use drinking-water purifiers) fall under ת"י 1505's mandatory scope, and whether "מה שטוב לאירופה" standards-reform changes (2022+) altered 1505's status. Check the SII standard page and the Ministry of Economy's standardization commissioner.
- Any water corporation (תאגיד מים וביוב) rules or municipal by-laws (חוקי עזר) that restrict household softener brine. None were found; check כללי תאגידי מים וביוב on Nevo and the local corporation.
- Whether the Health Ministry requires a separate approval (אישור משרד הבריאות) for home softeners beyond the standard. No such requirement was found.
- No Consumer Protection Authority decision or class action specifically on magnetic or electronic anti-scale devices was found.

## 3. Installation: licensed plumber, permits, backflow prevention, condominium consent

### Takeaway
I found no source establishing a general licensing requirement for plumbers in Israel, and none was found requiring a permit to install a point-of-entry softener or filter inside a home. Two regimes may apply at the edges. First, the backflow-prevention regulations (מז"ח, 1992) require devices installed by Health-Ministry-certified installers in certain connections. Second, any new electrical outlet or circuit is electrical work requiring a licensed electrician. Condominium consent is needed when equipment occupies or alters common property (roof, shared pipes, common areas). The only rule verified concerns solar heaters.

### Cited Findings
- תקנות בריאות העם (התקנת מכשיר מונע זרימת מים חוזרת), התשנ"ב-1992 set the duty to install backflow preventers. Installation must be done by installers certified by the Health Ministry (a list is on the ministry website). Businesses must install a device at the head of their water system and test it once a year — [Mei Carmel](https://www.mei-carmel.co.il/%D7%94%D7%A0%D7%93%D7%A1%D7%94/%D7%9E%D7%96%D7%97-%D7%9E%D7%9B%D7%A9%D7%99%D7%A8-%D7%9E%D7%95%D7%A0%D7%A2-%D7%96%D7%A8%D7%99%D7%9E%D7%94-%D7%97%D7%95%D7%96%D7%A8%D7%AA/%D7%94%D7%AA%D7%A7%D7%A0%D7%AA-%D7%9E%D7%96%D7%97-%D7%9E%D7%9B%D7%A9%D7%99%D7%A8-%D7%9E%D7%95%D7%A0%D7%A2-%D7%96%D7%A8%D7%99%D7%9E%D7%94-%D7%97%D7%95%D7%96%D7%A8%D7%AA/); [Kolchei Golan](https://kolhey.golan.org.il/%D7%9E%D7%95%D7%A0%D7%A2-%D7%96%D7%A8%D7%99%D7%9E%D7%94-%D7%97%D7%95%D7%96%D7%A8%D7%AA/)
- In private and condominium homes, the cited possible contamination sources are fire-sprinkler systems and fertigation systems (מערכות דישון) in the yard, balcony or home — [Kolchei Golan](https://kolhey.golan.org.il/%D7%9E%D7%95%D7%A0%D7%A2-%D7%96%D7%A8%D7%99%D7%9E%D7%94-%D7%97%D7%95%D7%96%D7%A8%D7%AA/). This is directly relevant to garden fertigation installs.
- Softeners need space, an electrical point and a drain point — [Eco-bar](https://www.eco-bar.co.il/%D7%90%D7%99%D7%9A-%D7%9C%D7%91%D7%97%D7%95%D7%A8-%D7%9E%D7%A2%D7%A8%D7%9B%D7%AA-%D7%98%D7%99%D7%94%D7%95%D7%A8-%D7%9E%D7%99%D7%9D-%D7%9C%D7%91%D7%99%D7%AA/) (commercial)
- Electricity Law, 1954, s.6(א), quoted in secondary sources: "לא יעסוק אדם בביצוע עבודת חשמל, אלא אם יש בידו רשיון מאת המנהל" — [pro.co.il](https://www.pro.co.il/electricians/guide/ask-for-electrician-license); [otg.co.il](https://otg.co.il/electric-work/)
- **Condominium (analogous rule for solar heaters, Land Law s.59א per Kol Zchut):** an apartment owner may install a solar water heater on the common roof without the other owners' consent if there is room for one heater per apartment. Otherwise consent of all owners is needed. A certificate from the local committee engineer and a planning permit are required. The Land Registration Superintendent (המפקח על רישום המקרקעין) can override an unreasonable objector — [Kol Zchut: התקנת דוד שמש בבית משותף](https://www.kolzchut.org.il/he/%D7%94%D7%AA%D7%A7%D7%A0%D7%AA_%D7%93%D7%95%D7%93_%D7%A9%D7%9E%D7%A9_%D7%91%D7%91%D7%99%D7%AA_%D7%9E%D7%A9%D7%95%D7%AA%D7%A3)

### Inferences
- A softener or filter installed on the apartment's own supply line after its meter, inside the apartment or the owner's private yard, is likely a private alteration needing no permit and no neighbour consent. Installing it on shared risers, on the roof, in a common storage room or stairwell, or draining brine into a shared line in a way that affects others touches common property (רכוש משותף), and consent or a general-assembly decision may be needed.
- For the middleman: installer quality is unregulated, so the brand's contractual and tort liability for leaks or water damage is the main exposure, not licensing. If a power outlet must be added, it must be done by a licensed electrician.

### Gaps
- No authoritative source was found confirming that plumbing has no licensing requirement in Israel. Check the Ministry of Labor's occupational regulation unit (אגף אסדרת עיסוקים).
- Whether a household softener connection requires a מז"ח was not verified. Check the 1992 regulations' schedule on Nevo.
- No condominium-law ruling specific to water softeners was found.

## 4. Business licence (צו רישוי עסקים (עסקים טעוני רישוי), התשע"ג-2013)

### Takeaway
The current order (in force 5.11.2013, amended including 2017 and 2022) lists "הדברה" as item 3.3: 3.3א sanitary pest control and 3.3ב agricultural pest control. I found no item for an office or online broker, a gardening service, or a water-treatment installer. This is an inference from the item lists in the search results, not a full read of the schedule.

### Cited Findings
- The order took effect on ב' בכסלו התשע"ד (5 November 2013). Businesses requiring a licence are those listed in the schedule, with columns marking which authorities must approve and which items get a fast-track permit (היתר מזורז, marked "א'") — [gov.il full consolidated text](https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/dynamiccollectorresultitem/legislation-011/he/info-page_legislation_legislation-1--011.pdf); [gov.il Interior Ministry guide PDF (2022)](https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/generalpage/regulation-law/he/Guides_regulation-law_24.07.2022.pdf)
- Item 3.3 "הדברה": 3.3א sanitary pest control (הדברה תברואתית); 3.3ב agricultural pest control, including by aircraft and other means. Item 3.4 covers pesticides and poisons for agricultural use. Amendment No. 2 (התשפ"ב-2022) added an accessibility-proof column — [Ganei Tikva consolidated order PDF](https://www.ganeytikva.org.il/uploads/n/1563173672.5605.pdf&gsessionid=yA-6OTRCCmgqOnFpymVOSQpJILZuh_ay_ODz3vcayktbh0faLsd0ig); [gov.il guide](https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/generalpage/regulation-law/he/Guides_regulation-law_24.07.2022.pdf)
- The older תקנות רישוי עסקים (הדברת מזיקים), תשל"ה-1975 require a permitted exterminator in every branch of a pest-control business — [Nevo](https://www.nevo.co.il/law_html/law01/p212m1_012.htm)

### Inferences
- A home-based or online sales and brokerage business with no premises open to the public, storage of hazardous materials or vehicles fleet likely needs no רישיון עסק. A gardening service and a softener installer likely need none either, unless they keep a warehouse of hazardous materials (pesticides, fuels) or the like.
- A pest-control business (item 3.3) needs a business licence in addition to the exterminator licence.

### Gaps
- The full schedule was not read line by line, because the fetch was blocked. Confirm against the gov.il consolidated text that there is no item for a "warehouse of chemicals or fertilizers", "storage of salt or water-treatment chemicals", or "workshop" that a softener business with stock could trigger.
- Municipal arnona or zoning rules for running a business from a residential apartment are outside this note.

## 5. Pesticides and fertilizers in private gardens

### Takeaway
Pest control against pests of people and property is regulated by חוק הסדרת העיסוק בהדברה תברואית, התשע"ו-2016, in force 1 June 2016 and administered by the Ministry of Environmental Protection. It prohibits anyone from engaging in "הדברה" without an exterminator licence of the matching type, and it excludes agricultural pest control. A gardener or garden company spraying insecticides or rodenticides against household pests (ants, mosquitoes, cockroaches, rodents) in a client's garden very likely needs a licensed מדביר. Whether treating plant pests on ornamental plants is "הדברה תברואית" or falls outside the law is unclear.

### Cited Findings
- The law came into force on כ"ד באייר התשע"ו (1 June 2016) and regulates pest control of pests to people and property. It does not apply to agricultural pest control. Quoted in search snippet: "לא יעסוק אדם בהדברה אלא אם כן יש בידו רישיון מדביר שניתן לו לפי חוק זה מסוג המתאים לביצוע ההדברה" — [Nevo: חוק הסדרת העיסוק בהדברה תברואית](https://www.nevo.co.il/law_html/law00/140423.htm); [Reshumot, Sefer HaChukim 2531, 16.2.2016](https://fs.knesset.gov.il/20/law/20_lsr_321784.pdf)
- The law replaces the earlier regime under the 1975 pest-control business-licensing regulations, with transition provisions for holders of a special permit — [MoEP guide for local authorities (March 2016)](https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/guide/exterminators_law/he/local-authrities-exterminators-law.pdf)
- The Ministry of Environmental Protection licenses exterminators, approves materials and publishes a portal of valid licensees — [MoEP pest-control department](https://www.gov.il/he/departments/units/pest-control-department)
- A training college says the home exterminator licence covers residential apartments or offices up to 500 m². For open areas it allows only assisting a higher-level exterminator. Training is 111 theory + 60 practical hours (Kinneret), and the licence is valid five years — [Mishlav college](https://mishlav.co.il/%D7%9C%D7%9E%D7%94-%D7%97%D7%A9%D7%95%D7%91-%D7%9C%D7%91%D7%A6%D7%A2-%D7%94%D7%93%D7%91%D7%A8%D7%94-%D7%A8%D7%A7-%D7%A2%D7%9C-%D7%99%D7%93%D7%99-%D7%9E%D7%99-%D7%A9%D7%94%D7%95%D7%A1%D7%9E%D7%9A/); [Kinneret](https://360.kinneret.ac.il/industry/pest-control/) (secondary)
- Only products approved for home use may be used by non-licensees; professional preparations are reserved for licence holders — [MoEP pest-control department](https://www.gov.il/he/departments/units/pest-control-department) (per search summary)

### Inferences
- **Middleman:** the law prohibits "engaging in" pest control without a licence. A brand that sells "ריסוס גינה" and dispatches an unlicensed gardener exposes both. A brand that dispatches a licensed exterminator is on safer ground, but whether a non-licensee may "engage in" the business of selling pest-control services is not verified. Pest control also needs a business licence (item 3.3).
- Fertilizing a garden: no licensing requirement was found (not researched in depth). Fertigation equipment connected to the water supply triggers the backflow-prevention rules (see section 3).

### Gaps
- The statutory definition of "הדברה"/"מזיק" in s.1 was not read, so it is unknown whether plant pests (aphids, scale insects, fungal disease) in private ornamental gardens fall under the 2016 law or under plant-protection legislation (Ministry of Agriculture). Check s.1 on Nevo and ask the MoEP pest unit.
- Whether a company (not an individual) may hold the licence or employ licensees, and the sanctions and fines under the 2016 law, were not researched.

## 6. Trees: פקודת היערות permits and fines

### Takeaway
Cutting or moving a "mature tree" (עץ בוגר) or a protected tree requires a licence from the forestry officer (פקיד היערות) under s.15(א) of the Forests Ordinance. A court ruling quotes the definition of a mature tree as at least 2 m tall with a trunk diameter of at least 10 cm at 130 cm above ground. Heavy pruning is reported to count as cutting. Unlicensed cutting is a criminal offence, reported at up to six months' imprisonment or a fine. Reported fines in practice run to about 7,000–8,000 NIS per case, and secondary sites cite up to 15,000 NIS per tree (unverified).

### Cited Findings
- s.15(א), quoted in search snippet: "לא יכרות אדם אילן מוגן או עץ בוגר ... אלא אם כן קיבל תחילה רישיון לכך מפקיד היערות". The officer will not license cutting unless satisfied that replacement trees will be planted, and may require a bank guarantee — [Nevo: פקודת היערות](https://www.nevo.co.il/law_html/law01/180_001.htm); [Kol Zchut: בקשת רישיון לכריתה או העתקה](https://www.kolzchut.org.il/he/%D7%91%D7%A7%D7%A9%D7%AA_%D7%A8%D7%99%D7%A9%D7%99%D7%95%D7%9F_%D7%9C%D7%9B%D7%A8%D7%99%D7%AA%D7%94_%D7%90%D7%95_%D7%94%D7%A2%D7%AA%D7%A7%D7%94_%D7%A9%D7%9C_%D7%A2%D7%A5_%D7%91%D7%95%D7%92%D7%A8_%D7%90%D7%95_%D7%A2%D7%A5_%D7%9E%D7%95%D7%92%D7%9F)
- Definition of "עץ בוגר" as quoted in a court decision: height at least 2 m and trunk diameter at least 10 cm measured 130 cm above ground. Cutting a non-mature tree needs no licence — [jacobdromi blog quoting a ruling](https://jacobdromi.wordpress.com/2024/04/10/krita-anish/) (secondary)
- Massive pruning of most of the tree is treated as cutting — [Rosenberg agronomy guide](https://rosenberg-agronom.com/%D7%A1%D7%A7%D7%A8-%D7%A2%D7%A6%D7%99%D7%9D/%D7%90%D7%99%D7%A9%D7%95%D7%A8-%D7%9B%D7%A8%D7%99%D7%AA%D7%AA-%D7%A2%D7%A5/) (secondary; statutory basis not verified)
- Penalty: unlicensed cutting is a criminal offence with a maximum of six months' imprisonment or a fine — [Knesset Research and Information Center](https://fs.knesset.gov.il/globaldocs/MMM/b846afe7-1981-e911-80f1-00155d0a9536/2_b846afe7-1981-e911-80f1-00155d0a9536_11_13703.pdf). A commercial guide cites s.17(7), with up to three years or a double fine where serious environmental damage results — [hamekarsem guide](https://www.hamekarsem.co.il/guides/tree-felling-permit) (secondary; section not verified). Rulings cited include fines of 7,000 and 8,000 NIS. A figure of "up to 15,000 NIS per tree" appears on secondary sites without a statutory anchor — [ha-makom](https://www.ha-makom.co.il/post-galit-tree-cuts/); [dinrega](https://www.dinrega.com/gs/%D7%94%D7%95%D7%93%D7%90%D7%94%20%D7%91%D7%A2%D7%91%D7%99%D7%A8%D7%AA%20%D7%9B%D7%A8%D7%99%D7%AA%D7%AA%20%D7%A2%D7%A6%D7%99%D7%9D%20%D7%9C%D7%9C%D7%90%20%D7%A8%D7%99%D7%A9%D7%99%D7%95%D7%9F%20(%D7%A4%D7%A7%D7%95%D7%93%D7%AA%20%D7%94%D7%99%D7%A2%D7%A8%D7%95%D7%AA))
- **Procedure and timing (secondary):** the request is made to the forestry officer at the local authority. Decision within 21 days, then 14 days from publication of the decision before the licence takes effect (an objection window). Licence valid two years. Fee reported as 58 NIS (2026) on one site and 55 NIS on another — [hamekarsem guide](https://www.hamekarsem.co.il/guides/tree-felling-permit); [Kol Zchut](https://www.kolzchut.org.il/he/%D7%91%D7%A7%D7%A9%D7%AA_%D7%A8%D7%99%D7%A9%D7%99%D7%95%D7%9F_%D7%9C%D7%9B%D7%A8%D7%99%D7%AA%D7%94_%D7%90%D7%95_%D7%94%D7%A2%D7%AA%D7%A7%D7%94_%D7%A9%D7%9C_%D7%A2%D7%A5_%D7%91%D7%95%D7%92%D7%A8_%D7%90%D7%95_%D7%A2%D7%A5_%D7%9E%D7%95%D7%92%D7%9F)

### Inferences
- The offence attaches to whoever "כורת" (cuts) the tree. A middleman that sells "כריתת עץ" or "גיזום" and dispatches a crew without a licence creates exposure for the crew and possibly for the brand as a party to the offence. The homeowner is the natural applicant, so the brand should make "licence obtained" a precondition of the job. At least about 35 days must be built into scheduling (21 + 14 days per the secondary source).

### Gaps
- The exact current definition of "עץ בוגר" in the ordinance, after the 2016 amendment (תיקון 5?), and the penalty section and fine amount were not verified from the statute. Check Nevo.
- Whether "heavy pruning = cutting" is statutory or interpretive was not verified.

## 7. Garden irrigation restrictions currently in force

### Takeaway
I could not verify any 2025–2026 Water Authority irrigation restriction currently in force for private gardens. The historical regime is כללי המים (השימוש במים באזור קיצוב), תשל"ו-1976 (sprinklers limited by hours, drip allowed any time) plus drought-era measures of 2008–2013. These may be outdated.

### Cited Findings
- כללי המים (השימוש במים באזור קיצוב), תשל"ו-1976 (search summary of the Nevo text): sprinkler irrigation of public and private gardens in April–November only between 17:00 and 10:00 next morning. "לעניין השקיה בטפטוף, רשאי אדם לצרוך מים להשקיה בחודשים האמורים אף שלא בשעות האמורות". The Nevo page shows several historical versions of the hours — [Nevo](https://www.nevo.co.il/law_html/law01/235_035.htm). Status today (whether an "אזור קיצוב" declaration is in force) is **not verified**.
- 2009: administrative fines of 250 NIS for a private garden owner irrigating in forbidden hours and 1,000 NIS for a municipality or public corporation — [Globes, 2009](https://www.globes.co.il/news/article.aspx?did=692663) (dated)
- A standing rule from 2001 is reported to bar irrigating public gardens, and private gardens larger than 50 m², unless drip irrigation with water meters and timers is installed — [HaMetaher](https://www.hametaher.co.il/water-wastewater-environmental/the-water-crisis/a-new-regulation-prohibiting-the-irrigation-of-private-and-public-gardens-2) (secondary, dated)
- Ynet reported that the Water Authority called for halting irrigation of public gardens during a desalination-plant shutdown; date not established — [ynet](https://www.ynet.co.il/environment-science/article/rynckam00fg)

### Inferences
- For a gardening brand, the practical rule is to design drip irrigation with timers, sprinklers only in evening and night hours, and to check the Water Authority's current notices each season.

### Gaps
- No current (2025/2026) official Water Authority page on household irrigation rules was retrieved. Check water.gov.il / gov.il Water Authority notices.

## 8. Comparison: regulatory burden of other home-service verticals and middleman exposure

### Takeaway
The verticals split cleanly. Electrical work, gas work, pest control, moving with heavy trucks (≥10 t), and from 15 Jan 2027 all AC installation and maintenance, carry personal occupational licences with criminal sanctions. Above-threshold construction-type work (renovation, hardscape, pergolas on foundations, likely waterproofing) carries contractor registration. Cleaning, ordinary gardening, artificial grass and handyman jobs under the threshold carry no specific licence. Two statutes found use language that reaches whoever provides the service through others. The Moving Services Law says "בעצמו או באמצעות אחר" explicitly. The Contractors Law turns on who "מקבל על עצמו" the work. Those are the clearest middleman exposures.

### Cited Findings (by vertical)
- **Electrical work:** Electricity Law, 1954, s.6(א): no one may engage in electrical work without a licence from the Director, matching the type of work. A breach is punishable by one year's imprisonment or a fine (s.11 per secondary sources; one older page gives 19,300 NIS, dated) — [pro.co.il](https://www.pro.co.il/electricians/guide/ask-for-electrician-license); [otg.co.il](https://otg.co.il/electric-work/). A "קבלן חשמל" licence category is discussed by an electrician's site — [amitmatan](https://amitmatan.co.il/%D7%A8%D7%99%D7%A9%D7%99%D7%95%D7%9F-%D7%A7%D7%91%D7%9C%D7%9F-%D7%97%D7%A9%D7%9E%D7%9C/) (content not verified)
- **Gas (LPG):** engaging in LPG work without the appropriate certification is an offence. There are installer levels 1 and 2 (level 1 needs at least 6 months' supervised practice; level 2 needs 2 years as level 1). Fees: 182 NIS new licence, 146 NIS renewal; renewal must be filed at least 60 days before expiry — [gov.il: בקשה לרישוי עיסוק בגפ"מ](https://www.gov.il/he/service/lpg_licensing_permits_and_inspection). Natural-gas work licensing regulations (טיוטת תקנות הגז (בטיחות ורישוי) (רישוי עוסקים בעבודות גז טבעי...), התשפ"ו-2025) were discussed in a Knesset committee in Feb 2026; status unknown — [Knesset committee draft](https://fs.knesset.gov.il/25/Committees/25_cs_bg_11191779.docx)
- **AC installation and cleaning/maintenance:** חוק הסדרת העיסוק בעבודה במערכת קירור או מיזוג אוויר, התשפ"ה-2025 was approved January 2025 and in force 15 July 2025. A licence is already required for work on systems using the flammable refrigerants listed in the law, and from 15 January 2027 a Ministry of Labor licence will be required for all installation and maintenance of refrigeration and AC systems, including residential — [Emess, Nov 2026](https://www.emess.co.il/radio/1958803); [Wikisource law text](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/%D7%97%D7%95%D7%A7_%D7%94%D7%A1%D7%93%D7%A8%D7%AA_%D7%94%D7%A2%D7%99%D7%A1%D7%95%D7%A7_%D7%91%D7%A2%D7%91%D7%95%D7%93%D7%94_%D7%91%D7%9E%D7%A2%D7%A8%D7%9B%D7%AA_%D7%A7%D7%99%D7%A8%D7%95%D7%A8_%D7%90%D7%95_%D7%9E%D7%99%D7%96%D7%95%D7%92_%D7%90%D7%95%D7%95%D7%99%D7%A8); [oknesset](https://oknesset.org/meetings/2/2/2223401.html). The electrical side of an AC unit already requires an electrician licence (חשמלאי שירות with a refrigeration/AC specialty) — [Israel Institute for Occupational Safety and Hygiene](https://www.osh.org.il/heb/questionlobby/weekanswer,465/)
- **Pest control:** exterminator licence under the 2016 law, plus business licence item 3.3. See sections 4–5.
- **Cleaning:** no occupational licence found (no source; inference).
- **Waterproofing (איטום):** no specific occupational licence found. Contractor registration applies if the work is "בנייה" above the threshold. The branch or sub-branch for waterproofing was **not verified**.
- **Solar water heaters (דודי שמש):** ת"י 579 covers solar water-heating systems (579.1 collectors, 579.2 tanks). Under the Planning and Building Law an installation check per ת"י 579 is required as part of the Form 4 (occupancy) documentation for new buildings — [SII solar testing](https://www.sii.org.il/he/lobby/lab-tests-search/laboratory-test/solar-testing/). The Ministry of Labor's occupational regulation unit published an analysis of boiler installation work (Dec 2021); its licensing conclusion was not retrieved — [gov.il PDF](https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/reports/research-papers-registration-and-licensing/he/prof-licensure_boiler-installation-011221.pdf). Condominium roof rules: Land Law s.59א per Kol Zchut (section 3 above).
- **Artificial grass:** no specific regulation found.
- **Pergolas:** permit exemption up to 50 m² or a quarter of the unbuilt area, with conditions. Contractor registration applies if the work is building work above the threshold or structural (section 1).
- **Moving:** חוק שירותי הובלה, התשנ"ז-1997 prohibits giving a transport service, "בעצמו או באמצעות אחר", without a carrier licence (רישיון מוביל). The licence requirement applies to commercial vehicles with a permitted total weight of 10,000 kg or more (any weight for hazardous materials). The licence lasts one year, is not transferable, and requires an approved course — [Nevo: חוק שירותי הובלה](https://www.nevo.co.il/law_html/law01/p221k41_001.htm); [Masa Batuach](https://www.maasa.co.il/%D7%91%D7%98%D7%99%D7%97%D7%95%D7%AA-%D7%91%D7%AA%D7%A2%D7%91%D7%95%D7%A8%D7%94/%D7%97%D7%95%D7%A7-%D7%A9%D7%99%D7%A8%D7%95%D7%AA%D7%99-%D7%94%D7%95%D7%91%D7%9C%D7%94-%D7%A8%D7%99%D7%A9%D7%99%D7%95%D7%9F-%D7%9E%D7%95%D7%91%D7%99%D7%9C/)
- **Handyman / renovation:** contractor sub-branch 131 שיפוצים exists. Registration is needed above the sub-branch threshold — [Nevo 1993 regs](https://www.nevo.co.il/law_html/law00/4779.htm)

### Inferences
- The lowest-burden verticals for a pure middleman are gardening maintenance, artificial grass, cleaning and point-of-use water filters with ת"י 1505-certified products. Hardscape, waterproofing and renovation become registration-bound above about 56,750 NIS per site (sub-branch), if the Nevo figure is current. Electrical, gas, AC (from 2027) and pest control require that every job be performed by a licensee, and they carry unresolved questions about whether the selling entity itself must be licensed.
- A structural mitigation across all verticals: the licensed or registered provider is the contracting party on the work order, and the brand acts as a disclosed marketer or agent. This reduces licensing exposure but changes the margin model and consumer-law disclosures; consumer-law aspects are covered by another researcher.

### Gaps
- Whether a non-licensed company may contract to provide electrical, gas, AC or pest-control services through licensed subcontractors was not verified for any vertical. Each needs checking against the statute's operative verb ("יעסוק", "יבצע", "ייתן שירות").
- No source was found on cleaning or handyman licensing; the absence of a licence is inferred, not confirmed.
- Waterproofing contractor sub-branch and any solar-heater installer licence were not verified.
