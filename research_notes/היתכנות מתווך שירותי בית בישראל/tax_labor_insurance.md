# Tax, labor and insurance rules for a "middleman" home-services business in Israel (as of November 2026)

Method note: research done 25.11.2026 with web search only. kolzchut.org.il and he.wikisource.org could not be opened from this session (network block), so no page was opened directly; every finding below comes from the text the search tool pulled from the cited URL. Where the only source is a commercial guide or a law-firm blog, the finding is marked "secondary". "לא אומת / not verified" means no source was found and the item must be checked at the named place. This is general information, not tax or legal advice.

## 1. Business form, registration, and the 2026 headline rates

### Takeaway
A single owner can start as עוסק פטור (2026 turnover ceiling NIS 122,833), but a middleman who books the full job price as his own turnover will cross that ceiling at roughly NIS 10,000 of sales a month, so in practice the realistic forms are עוסק מורשה or a חברה בע"מ. A company can never be עוסק פטור. 2026 headline rates: VAT 18%, corporate tax 23%. Self-employed ביטוח לאומי + health tax is 7.7% up to NIS 7,703 a month and 18% above it.

### Cited Findings
**עוסק פטור ceiling and eligibility**
- The עוסק פטור turnover ceiling is NIS 122,833 for 2026, up from NIS 120,000 in 2025. The Tax Authority's online service page for opening a פטור file states "בשנת 2026 – 122,833 ₪". — [gov.il: בקשה לפתיחת תיק עוסק פטור באמצעות האינטרנט](https://www.gov.il/he/service/request-open-exempt-dealer-via-internet); [Kol Zchut: עוסק פטור](https://www.kolzchut.org.il/he/עוסק_פטור) (search excerpt)
- The test is annual transaction turnover (מחזור עסקאות) before expenses, not profit. — [Bizportal (secondary)](https://www.bizportal.co.il/guides/news/article/20039167); [Kol Zchut](https://www.kolzchut.org.il/he/עוסק_פטור)
- VAT regulations, תקנה 13, list occupations that cannot register as פטור whatever their turnover. Per a 2024 secondary summary the list includes liberal professions (lawyers, accountants, engineers, tax advisers, management consultants, insurance agents, appraisers), real-estate dealers and brokers, car dealers and brokers, doctors, driving schools, **companies registered under the Companies Ordinance**, and cooperative societies. A general home-services intermediary does not appear on that list. — [Green Invoice magazine, "who can be exempt dealer" (secondary, 2024)](https://www.greeninvoice.co.il/magazine/who-can-be-exempt-dealer/). The official current text of תקנה 13 was not opened (לא אומת: check the תקנות מס ערך מוסף, התשל"ו-1976 on Nevo).
- A "בעל עסק זעיר" (micro-business) track for individuals has the same 2026 ceiling (NIS 122,833) and lets the owner deduct a fixed 30% of turnover instead of actual expenses (search summary). — [gov.il: בקשה לשינוי סוג תיק ל"בעל עסק זעיר"](https://www.gov.il/he/service/request-transfer-to-micro-business-owner)

**Registration steps and costs**
- **עוסק פטור:** the application is online and free. If a ביטוח לאומי file is needed, the Tax Authority system passes it on automatically, with no separate application. — [gov.il פטור service](https://www.gov.il/he/service/request-open-exempt-dealer-via-internet)
- **עוסק מורשה:** form 821, free of charge. The service page describes printing, signing and filing the form at the regional office by appointment, or through a licensed representative (מייצג). The documents are an ID, a bank-account confirmation or cancelled cheque, and a lease if relevant. — [gov.il: בקשה לפתיחת תיק עוסק מורשה (טופס 821)](https://www.gov.il/he/service/vat-821)
- A joint Tax Authority and ביטוח לאומי system, launched in July 2024, opens VAT, income-tax and withholding files and the ביטוח לאומי self-employed file in one place. It is self-service for פטור and goes through representatives for other dealers. — [Tax Authority letter, July 2024, via Capitax](https://capitax.co.il/Attachments/15072024.pdf); [ביטוח לאומי: פתיחת תיק עצמאי](https://www.btl.gov.il/Insurance/National%20Insurance/type_list/Self_Employed/Pages/howtoregister.aspx)
- **Company:** a company opens its income-tax and withholding file with form 4436. — [gov.il: פתיחת תיק לתאגיד (טופס 4436)](https://www.gov.il/he/service/itc4436)
- **Companies Registrar incorporation fee, 2026:** NIS 2,559 online and NIS 3,123 for a paper filing. — [Kamma (secondary)](https://kamma.co.il/company/fees); [Fait Law (secondary)](https://faitlaw.co.il/%D7%90%D7%92%D7%A8%D7%94-%D7%9C%D7%A4%D7%AA%D7%99%D7%97%D7%AA-%D7%97%D7%91%D7%A8%D7%94-2026-%D7%94%D7%9E%D7%93%D7%A8%D7%99%D7%9A-%D7%94%D7%9E%D7%9C%D7%90-%D7%95%D7%94%D7%9E%D7%A2%D7%95%D7%93%D7%9B/). One secondary source gives about NIS 2,770 instead: [Mena CPA](https://www.mena.co.il/agrat-rasham-hachavarot-2026-tashlum-agra-shnatit-madrich/). The official fee table was found but its text could not be parsed: [רשות התאגידים fee table](https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/reports/companies-fee-rates-2020/he/companies-fee-rates-2025.pdf).
- **Companies Registrar annual fee (אגרה שנתית), 2026:** NIS 1,338 reduced if paid by 31.03.2026, NIS 1,777 regular from 01.04.2026. The 2025 figures were NIS 1,306 and NIS 1,734. — [gov.il: תשלום אגרה שנתית לחברה או שותפות](https://www.gov.il/he/service/company_partnership_annual_payment); [RSM, 2025 fee](https://www.rsm.global/israel/he/insights/grh-sntyt-lrsm-hhbrwt-2025)

**Rates in force in 2026**
- **VAT:** 18%, raised from 17% on 1.1.2025. The 2026 budget was built on 18%; a proposal to raise VAT to 19% was not legislated. — [Bizportal VAT calculator 2026 (secondary)](https://www.bizportal.co.il/additives/301/vat-calculator-2026-.html). The Tax Authority's official page appeared in the results but its text was not extracted: [gov.il: סכומים ושיעורים במע"מ](https://www.gov.il/he/departments/general/vat-rate-amount-new).
- **Corporate tax:** 23% in 2026, unchanged from 2025. — [PwC Worldwide Tax Summaries, Israel](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/israel/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income); [Deloitte Israel tax highlights 2026](https://www.deloitte.com/content/dam/assets-shared/docs/services/tax/2026/dttl-tax-israelhighlights-2026.pdf)
- A 2026 "trapped profits" (רווחים כלואים) law was reported. Under it, companies that hold cash with no business use may pay an extra 2% a year. The details are not verified. — [Walla (secondary)](https://news.walla.co.il/item/3847680)
- **Self-employed ביטוח לאומי + health tax, 2026, below retirement age:**
  - Reduced rate of 7.7% (4.47% national insurance + 3.23% health) on monthly income up to NIS 7,703.
  - Full rate of 18% (12.83% + 5.17%) from NIS 7,703 up to the ceiling of NIS 51,910 a month.
  - Minimum payment of about NIS 265 a month.
  — [ביטוח לאומי: שיעורי דמי הביטוח לעצמאים](https://www.btl.gov.il/Insurance/Rates/Pages/לעצמאים.aspx); [ביטוח לאומי 2026 advances circular](https://www.btl.gov.il/Insurance/HozrimBituah/Hozrim/_%d7%9e%d7%a7%d7%93%d7%9e%d7%95%d7%aa_%d7%95%d7%93%d7%9e%d7%99_%d7%91%d7%99%d7%98%d7%95%d7%97_%d7%9c%d7%a2%d7%a6%d7%9e%d7%90%d7%99%d7%9d_2026__.pdf)
- For 2026–2028 the reduced-rate threshold is CPI-linked each 1 January instead of being set at 60% of the average wage, which is why NIS 7,703 is below 60% of the 2026 average wage. Two ביטוח לאומי documents give different January 2026 average wages, NIS 13,566 and NIS 13,769. — [ביטוח לאומי annual survey 2025, ch. 4](https://www.btl.gov.il/Publications/Skira_shnatit/2025/Documents/chap-4-geviya.pdf)

### Inferences
- A עוסק פטור middleman who invoices the full job price as his own sale reaches NIS 122,833 at about NIS 10,200 of gross sales a month, roughly 3–6 garden jobs or 2–3 softener installations. If he works as a disclosed agent (section 2), only his commissions should count as his turnover, which would keep him under the ceiling far longer. Confirm this with an accountant.
- The בעל עסק זעיר fixed 30% deduction is a poor fit for a principal-model middleman, whose real costs (subcontractor payments) are likely 60–80% of turnover.
- A חברה בע"מ cannot be פטור. It costs the incorporation fee, the NIS 1,777 annual fee and higher accounting fees (section 8), and it adds dividend tax on distributions. The dividend rate was not verified.

### Gaps
- The 2026 income-tax brackets for individuals and the dividend tax rate for a material shareholder (commonly 30%, plus surtax) were not verified. Check the Tax Authority's 2026 "נקודות זיכוי ומדרגות מס" page or the PwC/Deloitte 2026 summaries.
- The official 2026 incorporation fee should be checked on the רשות התאגידים fee table, since secondary sources disagree.
- Whether any 2026 legislation changed the VAT rate after mid-2026: no source shows a change, but the gov.il rate page was not opened.

## 2. VAT on the margin: principal model vs. agent (שלוח/סוכן) model

### Takeaway
Israeli VAT law (חוק מס ערך מוסף, התשל"ו-1975, סעיף 17) gives the middleman agent treatment only if he acts *in the name of* the subcontractor and the documents given to the customer state the agency and the subcontractor's name. VAT is then due only on his commission, and the job itself is the subcontractor's transaction. If he sells in his own name, the law treats it as two sales: VAT on the full price, with input VAT deductible only from a עוסק מורשה subcontractor's valid tax invoice. A Tel Aviv District Court ruling rejected an intermediary's claim to pay VAT only on its margin. Agent treatment also means the brand cannot present itself to the customer as the service provider, which conflicts with the planned "looks like a service company" model.

### Cited Findings
- **סעיף 17(א), disclosed agency (שליחות גלויה):** a dealer who sells an asset or gives a service to a buyer through an agent acting in the dealer's name is treated as the seller or service provider to the buyer, and the agent as giving a service to the dealer. The condition is that the fact of the agency and the dealer's name appear on the documents the agent issues to the buyer. Otherwise the transaction is treated differently. — [Nevo: חוק מס ערך מוסף, התשל"ו-1975](https://www.nevo.co.il/law_html/law01/271_001.htm) (search extract of the section text)
- **סעיף 17(ב), undisclosed agency, selling in one's own name:** "עוסק המוכר בשם עצמו נכס של פלוני, שנתקשר עמו לשם כך, רואים את העוסק כמוכר הנכס לקונה ואת פלוני כמוכר הנכס לעוסק". That is, two taxable transactions. — [Nevo](https://www.nevo.co.il/law_html/law01/271_001.htm); [Nevo, older consolidated text](https://www.nevo.co.il/law_html/law00/72813.htm) (search extracts). Note: the extracted wording of 17(ב) mentions "נכס" (an asset). Whether and how it applies to services was not verified (לא אומת); read the full section 17 on Nevo.
- **Case law:** in ע"מ 55957-10-23 (Tel Aviv District Court), an intermediary between suppliers and customers argued that it owed VAT only on its margin (commission). The court appears to have rejected the argument, holding under 17(ב) that in an undisclosed agency the selling dealer must report the transaction as its own. Only part of the judgment was extracted; read it in full. — [Capitax: ע"מ 55957-10-23 (PDF)](https://www.capitax.co.il/Attachments/55957-10-23.pdf)
- The Supreme Court held that a brokerage commission an Israeli intermediary received from a foreign client bears full VAT when the service is given at the same time to an Israeli party. This is relevant to how broadly courts read "service" in brokerage, though not to agency as such. — [Dvorin law firm summary, 20.9.2025 (secondary)](https://dvorin-law.co.il/2025/09/20/%D7%A4%D7%A1%D7%A7-%D7%93%D7%99%D7%9F-%D7%AA%D7%A7%D7%93%D7%99%D7%9E%D7%99-%D7%91%D7%A2%D7%9C%D7%99%D7%95%D7%9F-%D7%A2%D7%9E%D7%9C%D7%AA-%D7%AA%D7%99%D7%95%D7%95%D7%9A-%D7%9E%D7%9C%D7%A7%D7%95%D7%97/)
- **סעיף 18** (a different rule): when an enforcement officer, liquidator, receiver or guardian sells another person's assets by virtue of office, the owner is the seller and the office-holder a service provider. It does not apply here. — [Nevo](https://www.nevo.co.il/law_html/law01/271_001.htm) (search extract)
- **Self-invoice (חשבונית עצמית)** is allowed only in cases listed in the VAT regulations: services from a person whose main income is salary or pension, for listed services only (תקנה 6א(א)); a foreign-resident seller; real estate from a non-dealer. None of these covers buying from a עוסק פטור gardener. — [Protocol (secondary)](https://protocol.co.il/self-invoice/)
- A עוסק פטור charges no VAT, so buying from one normally comes with no tax invoice carrying VAT. — [Protocol (secondary)](https://protocol.co.il/self-invoice/)
- Input-VAT deduction on a tax invoice above the חשבוניות ישראל threshold also requires an allocation number (section 4). — [gov.il: בקשה למספר הקצאה לחשבונית מס](https://www.gov.il/he/service/request-assignment-number-for-tax-invoice)

### Inferences
These are arithmetic illustrations, not sourced figures. Assume a job priced at NIS 1,000 before VAT and a subcontractor cost of NIS 700.
- **Principal model, subcontractor is עוסק מורשה:**
  - The customer pays NIS 1,180. The middleman owes NIS 180 output VAT.
  - The subcontractor charges NIS 700 + 126 VAT, and the middleman deducts the NIS 126 as input VAT.
  - Net VAT is NIS 54, which is 18% of the NIS 300 margin. The margin is intact.
- **Principal model, subcontractor is עוסק פטור (charges a flat NIS 700, no VAT):**
  - The middleman owes NIS 180 output VAT with no input credit. Net VAT is 18% of the *whole* NIS 1,000.
  - The middleman still keeps NIS 300 only if the customer accepts NIS 1,180.
  - A household can hire the same פטור gardener directly for NIS 700 with no VAT. The middleman's price is therefore structurally about 12.6% of the subcontractor's cost (NIS 126 here) higher than an agent-model or direct price.
  - If market pressure caps the price at NIS 1,000 *including* VAT, net revenue is NIS 847 and the margin falls from NIS 300 to about NIS 147. VAT absorbs roughly half the margin.
- **No invoice from the subcontractor:**
  - Same VAT result as the פטור case: no input credit.
  - The payment is also hard to support as a deductible expense for income tax, which raises taxable profit toward the full price.
  - It creates exposure for unreported payments and cash-limit breaches (section 5).
- **Agent model (סעיף 17(א)):**
  - The documents the customer receives must name the subcontractor as the provider and state that the brand acts as its agent.
  - The subcontractor issues the receipt or tax invoice to the customer: no VAT if פטור, 18% if מורשה.
  - The middleman invoices the subcontractor for the commission plus 18% VAT.
  - This removes the VAT drag on פטור subcontractors but requires disclosing the agency to the customer. It also shifts consumer-facing responsibility, a matter for the consumer-law researcher.
- Collecting the full price from the customer while presenting the brand as the provider looks like the undisclosed-agency or own-name scenario the district court taxed on the full amount.

### Gaps
- No Tax Authority circular or professional position paper was found that sets out conditions for agent treatment of *services* (as opposed to assets) under סעיף 17, or for a payment-collection agent (גבייה עבור אחר). Check the Tax Authority "הוראות ביצוע מע"מ" and "ניירות עמדה" pages and the full text of section 17 on Nevo.
- The full reasoning of ע"מ 55957-10-23, and whether it was appealed, was not verified.
- Whether a פטור subcontractor's turnover ceiling is affected when the middleman collects money on its behalf was not verified.

## 3. Withholding tax (ניכוי מס במקור) on payments to subcontractors

### Takeaway
The duty to withhold on payments for services applies only to "payers" defined in the withholding order: in 2026, those whose 2024 turnover exceeded NIS 5.3m, or who must keep double-entry books (secondary sources). A small עוסק מורשה middleman will usually not be a required withholder, but a company may be. Where withholding applies, the default is 30% unless the subcontractor has a certificate for a lower rate, and 20% applies when the payee shows he keeps proper books. Private household customers do not withhold.

### Cited Findings
- **Legal source:** תקנות מס הכנסה (ניכוי מתשלומים בעד שירותים או נכסים), התשל"ז-1977, made under sections 164 and 243 of פקודת מס הכנסה. The default rate is 30%. — [Nevo: התקנות](https://www.nevo.co.il/law_html/law01/255_374.htm) (search extract)
- The rate is 20% when the recipient shows confirmation that it kept acceptable books and filed returns; a lower or zero rate is available on application to the assessing officer. — [Israel Hayom (secondary)](https://www.israelhayom.co.il/article/658703)
- **Who must withhold (2026):** per the Tax Authority's official January 2026 withholding booklet, a payer whose turnover in tax year 2024 exceeded NIS 5,300,000. The booklet gives a carve-out for a low-turnover subsidiary, a one-off payment for services or assets exempt from withholding up to NIS 5,520, and a 30% rate for services and assets in 2026. — [Tax Authority, לוח עזר לחישוב מס הכנסה ממשכורת, ינואר 2026 (PDF)](https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/generalpage/income-tax-monthly-deductions-booklet/he/generalInformation_income-tax-monthly-deductions-booklet_monthly-deductions-booklet-2026.pdf) (search extract)
- The duty also applies to anyone required to keep double-entry books, whether an individual or a company. The order is צו מס הכנסה (קביעת תשלומים בעד שירותים או נכסים כהכנסה). — [Efraty CPA guide (secondary)](https://www.efraty.com/%D7%9E%D7%99%D7%93%D7%A2-%D7%A9%D7%99%D7%9E%D7%95%D7%A9%D7%99/%D7%9E%D7%A1-%D7%94%D7%9B%D7%A0%D7%A1%D7%94/%D7%A0%D7%99%D7%9B%D7%95%D7%99-%D7%9E%D7%A1-%D7%91%D7%9E%D7%A7%D7%95%D7%A8-%D7%9E%D7%AA%D7%A9%D7%9C%D7%95%D7%9D-%D7%A2%D7%91%D7%95%D7%A8-%D7%A9%D7%99%D7%A8%D7%95%D7%AA%D7%99%D7%9D-%D7%95-%D7%90%D7%95/); [Ziv Shiffer CPA (secondary)](https://www.zscpa.co.il/%D7%A0%D7%99%D7%9B%D7%95%D7%99-%D7%9E%D7%A1-%D7%91%D7%9E%D7%A7%D7%95%D7%A8-%D7%9E%D7%A9%D7%99%D7%A8%D7%95%D7%AA%D7%99%D7%9D-%D7%95%D7%A0%D7%9B%D7%A1%D7%99%D7%9D/). Secondary sources give different turnover figures (NIS 5m, 5.3m, 5.4m), probably from different years; the NIS 5.3m figure is from the official 2026 booklet.
- Tax Authority הוראת ביצוע 02/2026 reportedly requires every payer obliged to withhold to check suppliers' certificates shortly before each payment, free of charge on the Tax Authority site. — [Optipay (secondary, citing the instruction)](https://www.optipay.co.il/blog/withholding-tax)
- If a required withholder fails to withhold, the Tax Authority may demand the unwithheld tax plus interest and penalties, or disallow the expense. — [Ziv Shiffer CPA (secondary)](https://www.zscpa.co.il/%D7%A0%D7%99%D7%9B%D7%95%D7%99-%D7%9E%D7%A1-%D7%91%D7%9E%D7%A7%D7%95%D7%A8-%D7%9E%D7%A1%D7%A4%D7%A7%D7%99%D7%9D/) (attribution from search summary)
- The Tax Authority's information page on withholding and bookkeeping certificates (אישור ניכוי מס במקור, אישור ניהול ספרים) is information only, and the law prevails over it. — [gov.il: מידע על אישורי ניכוי מס במקור וניהול ספרים](https://www.gov.il/he/service/itc-gmishurim)
- **Older case law:** a 2015 Jerusalem Magistrate's Court decision held that the 30% withholding is mandatory and that section 164 leaves no real discretion. — [search extract; Israel Hayom article](https://www.israelhayom.co.il/article/658703)

### Inferences
- A sole-proprietor middleman with turnover well below NIS 5.3m and single-entry books is probably *not* a required withholder. Asking every subcontractor for an אישור ניהול ספרים and a withholding certificate is still prudent: it screens out off-the-books operators and documents that the payee is a real business, which also helps on the employment and foreign-worker risks in section 6.
- If the business is a company, the accountant should first confirm whether it must keep double-entry books and therefore withhold. If it must, any subcontractor without a certificate costs 30% of each payment in withholding, which pushes subcontractors to demand higher gross prices.

### Gaps
- The exact current wording of the withholding order and of the bookkeeping instructions (הוראות ניהול פנקסים) that decide when a small company must keep double-entry books was not verified. Check Nevo and the Tax Authority.
- The full text of הוראת ביצוע 02/2026 was not opened.

## 4. חשבוניות ישראל (allocation numbers) in 2026

### Takeaway
From 1 January 2026 a tax invoice above NIS 10,000 before VAT needed an allocation number for the business customer to deduct input VAT; from 1 June 2026 the threshold is NIS 5,000 before VAT. It matters only where the buyer is a עוסק claiming input VAT. B2C invoices to households do not need one. It does matter for a עוסק מורשה subcontractor's invoices of more than NIS 5,000 before VAT to the middleman.

### Cited Findings
- **Threshold schedule** (all amounts before VAT):

  | From | Threshold |
  |---|---|
  | May 2024 | NIS 25,000 |
  | January 2025 | NIS 20,000 |
  | 1 January 2026 | NIS 10,000 |
  | 1 June 2026 | NIS 5,000 |

  Example: an invoice of NIS 4,900 before VAT needs no number even though the total with VAT exceeds NIS 5,000. — [Green Invoice: Israel Invoice guide (secondary)](https://www.greeninvoice.co.il/magazine/israel-invoice/); [Green Invoice: requirements in force from 1.6.26](https://www.greeninvoice.co.il/israel-invoice/); [gov.il: בקשה למספר הקצאה לחשבונית מס](https://www.gov.il/he/service/request-assignment-number-for-tax-invoice)
- Allocation numbers are issued for tax invoices to customers registered as עוסק מורשה, and the number is a condition for the customer's input-VAT deduction above the threshold. — [gov.il service page](https://www.gov.il/he/service/request-assignment-number-for-tax-invoice); [PwC Israel tax news 23/2023](https://www.pwc.com/il/he/tax/tax-news-2023/2023/tax-news-2023-23.html)
- For a private (non-dealer) customer the number is not mandatory, though it can be requested voluntarily. — [PwC Israel tax news 23/2023](https://www.pwc.com/il/he/tax/tax-news-2023/2023/tax-news-2023-23.html); [Deloitte Israel tax alert 6/2024](https://www2.deloitte.com/content/dam/Deloitte/il/Documents/tax/tax_alerts/2024/6.2024.pdf) (search summary)
- Zero-rated invoices are excluded. — [State Comptroller, 2026 economic report](https://library.mevaker.gov.il/sites/DigitalLibrary/Documents/2026/Economic-Report/2026-Economic-Report-104.docx)
- A buyer can check a supplier's invoice details by allocation number on a Tax Authority service. — [gov.il: אימות פרטי חשבונית הספק על סמך מספר הקצאה](https://www.gov.il/he/service/verify-vendor-invoice-information)
- A secondary source claims that without an allocation number the invoice will also not be recognized as a deductible expense for income tax. This is not confirmed in a primary source and should be treated as unverified. — [Green Invoice (secondary)](https://www.greeninvoice.co.il/magazine/israel-invoice/)

### Inferences
- Typical household gardening jobs are small, but softener installations or garden makeovers could exceed NIS 5,000 before VAT.
- In the principal model, the middleman must make sure a מורשה subcontractor's invoice above NIS 5,000 carries an allocation number, or he loses the input VAT.
- A פטור subcontractor issues no tax invoice, so the rule is irrelevant there; the loss of input VAT is already total.
- The middleman's own invoices to households need no allocation number.

### Gaps
- Whether the June 2026 step was later deferred or tightened again (for example to NIS 0 in 2027) was not verified. Check the gov.il חשבוניות ישראל page.

## 5. Cash limits (חוק לצמצום השימוש במזומן, התשע"ח-2018) and paying subcontractors in cash or without invoices

### Takeaway
Since 1 August 2022 a business (עוסק) may pay or receive cash only up to NIS 6,000 per transaction. On a larger transaction the cash portion is capped at the lower of 10% of the price or NIS 6,000. Breaches carry administrative fines (עיצום כספי) set as a percentage of the breach, which secondary sources give as 15% to 30%, and artificial splitting of a payment can be criminal. Paying subcontractors in cash without invoices therefore combines a cash-law breach with lost VAT and income-tax deductions.

### Cited Findings
- The business cash ceiling fell from NIS 11,000 to NIS 6,000 on 1.8.2022. — [ynet](https://www.ynet.co.il/economy/article/bkyrd5r2q)
- Worked example: on a NIS 30,000 transaction at most NIS 3,000 may be paid in cash (the lower of NIS 6,000 and 10%). — [mako](https://www.mako.co.il/finances-money/Article-a68408882235281027.htm)
- The same ynet piece is summarised as saying "10% or NIS 15,000, the lower". That probably refers to non-business (individual-to-individual) transactions; this is a conflict to verify. — [ynet](https://www.ynet.co.il/economy/article/bkyrd5r2q)
- Wages above NIS 6,000 may not be paid in cash. — [mako](https://www.mako.co.il/finances-money/Article-a68408882235281027.htm) (search summary)
- **Administrative fines:** a Tax Authority instruction (inst-1-2024) gives 15% of the breach up to NIS 25,000, 20% for NIS 25,000–50,000, and 30% above NIS 50,000. — [Tax Authority instruction 1/2024 on the cash law (PDF)](https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/policy/inst-1-2024/he/ReduceTheUseOfCash_reduce-the-use-of-cash-110324.pdf). Older press items give 10% / 15% / 25%, possibly an earlier version. — [mako](https://www.mako.co.il/finances-money/Article-a68408882235281027.htm). Treat the official instruction as the better source; the rate tiers were not checked against the statute's schedule.
- Fines on a עוסק can reach 15–30% of the breach, and artificially splitting a transaction to evade the law may be a criminal offence. — [ice.co.il (secondary)](https://www.ice.co.il/law/news/article/981838)
- The Tax Authority publishes the fines it imposes under the law. — [gov.il: פרסום הטלת עיצומים כספיים לפי חוק צמצום השימוש במזומן](https://www.gov.il/he/service/publication-imposition-financial-sanctions)
- **2026 cheque rules:**
  - A June 2026 Tax Authority circular deals with an amendment on cashing cheques: a cheque above NIS 6,000 can be exchanged for cash only up to the lower of 10% or NIS 6,000. — [Tax Authority income-tax instruction 06/2026 (PDF)](https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/policy/inst-06-2026/he/IncomeTax_inst-06-2026.pdf)
  - The 2026 Economic Efficiency (Arrangements) bill included limits on discounting or cashing cheques above NIS 6,000, with cashing of NIS 6,000–25,000 allowed against a receipt or invoice. Its final enacted form was not confirmed. — [Knesset bill text (PDF)](https://fs.knesset.gov.il/25/law/25_ls_bk_11371595.pdf); [Chamber of Commerce update on the 2026 Arrangements Law](https://www.chamber.org.il/media/170261/%D7%97%D7%95%D7%A7-%D7%94%D7%94%D7%A1%D7%93%D7%A8%D7%99%D7%9D-2026-%D7%A2%D7%93%D7%9B%D7%95%D7%9F-%D7%9C%D7%97%D7%91%D7%A8%D7%99-%D7%94%D7%9C%D7%A9%D7%9B%D7%94_%D7%97%D7%93%D7%A9.pdf)

### Inferences
- Most single gardening visits fall under NIS 6,000, so occasional cash below that is lawful under the cash law. It still needs an invoice or receipt for VAT and income tax, and under the principal model a cash payment to a subcontractor with no invoice gives no input VAT and a weakly supported expense.
- Paying subcontractors by bank transfer or app, against a receipt or tax invoice, is the only clean route.
- Large softener or landscaping jobs above NIS 6,000 must be collected mostly by non-cash means.

### Gaps
- The statutory section numbers for the business cash limit and for the fine schedule were not verified. Check the law on Nevo or the Knesset site.
- The final status of the 2026 Arrangements Law cash and cheque provisions is not verified.

## 6. Employment-law risk: misclassification, manpower-contractor licensing, the Enforcement Law, and foreign or Palestinian workers

### Takeaway
Labour courts decide employee status by a "mixed test" weighted toward integration (השתלבות), whatever the contract says. A gardener who works only for the brand, under its schedule, pricing, uniform and control, could be held its employee. The manpower-contractor licence law targets businesses that supply their *own employees* to work for others. Service-contractor licensing and the client-liability chapter of the Enforcement Law cover only guarding, security, cleaning and (for client liability) catering. On the sources found, neither reaches gardening or softener installation. The sharpest legal risk is a subcontractor using Palestinian workers without permits or foreign workers without permits: secondary legal sources say the person who orders the work can face criminal liability, and a contract clause shifting responsibility does not protect against it.

### Cited Findings
**Employee status**
- Employee status is a legal determination under tests set in case law, not by the parties' agreement. A contract calling the relationship contractor and client does not defeat employee status where the substantive tests are met. — [Kol Zchut: קביעת קיומם של יחסי עובד-מעביד](https://www.kolzchut.org.il/he/קביעת_קיומם_של_יחסי_עובד-מעביד) (search excerpt)
- The National Labour Court applies a "mixed test" (מבחן מעורב) with strong emphasis on the integration test (מבחן ההשתלבות). It also weighs the supervision/control test and other sub-tests. — [Kol Zchut](https://www.kolzchut.org.il/he/קביעת_קיומם_של_יחסי_עובד-מעביד); [Protocol (secondary)](https://protocol.co.il/employer-employee-relationship/)

**Manpower contractors (חוק העסקת עובדים על ידי קבלני כוח אדם, התשנ"ו-1996)**
- "קבלן כוח אדם" is a person whose business is providing manpower services of *its employees* to work for another. "קבלן שירות" is a person whose business is providing a service in a field listed in the Second Schedule through its employees at another's premises. No one may act as a manpower contractor without a licence. — [Knesset law text (PDF)](https://fs.knesset.gov.il/13/law/13_lsr_211304.pdf); unofficial text via [Wikisource](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/%D7%97%D7%95%D7%A7_%D7%94%D7%A2%D7%A1%D7%A7%D7%AA_%D7%A2%D7%95%D7%91%D7%93%D7%99%D7%9D_%D7%A2%D7%9C_%D7%99%D7%93%D7%99_%D7%A7%D7%91%D7%9C%D7%A0%D7%99_%D7%9B%D7%95%D7%97_%D7%90%D7%93%D7%9D) (search extracts)
- The Ministry of Labor licenses "קבלן כוח אדם או קבלן שירות בתחומים שמירה, אבטחה וניקיון":
  - Experience required: 1 year of managerial or business experience for a service contractor, 3 years of manpower management for a manpower contractor.
  - A guarantee based on payroll is required.
  - Licences are renewable for up to 3 years.
  - Operating without a licence is an offence.
  — [gov.il: בקשה לרישיון קבלן כוח אדם או קבלן שירות](https://www.gov.il/he/service/manpower-contractor-license-request); [gov.il: חידוש רישיון](https://www.gov.il/he/service/manpower-contractor-license-renewal)

**Enforcement Law (חוק להגברת האכיפה של דיני העבודה, התשע"ב-2011, in force June 2012)**
- The client-liability rules for contractor workers cover three sectors: guarding/security, cleaning, and catering, the last only where it serves the client's own employees. — [Kol Zchut: חוק להגברת האכיפה של דיני העבודה](https://www.kolzchut.org.il/he/%D7%97%D7%95%D7%A7_%D7%9C%D7%94%D7%92%D7%91%D7%A8%D7%AA_%D7%94%D7%90%D7%9B%D7%99%D7%A4%D7%94_%D7%A9%D7%9C_%D7%93%D7%99%D7%A0%D7%99_%D7%94%D7%A2%D7%91%D7%95%D7%93%D7%94) (search excerpt); [Nevo law text](https://www.nevo.co.il/law_html/law00/117033.htm); [Hilan (secondary)](https://www.hilan.co.il/%D7%9E%D7%A8%D7%9B%D7%96-%D7%99%D7%93%D7%A2/%D7%91%D7%A1%D7%99%D7%A1-%D7%99%D7%93%D7%A2/%D7%9E%D7%90%D7%9E%D7%A8%D7%99%D7%9D/%D7%94%D7%97%D7%95%D7%A7-%D7%9C%D7%94%D7%92%D7%91%D7%A8%D7%AA-%D7%94%D7%90%D7%9B%D7%99%D7%A4%D7%94-%D7%A9%D7%9C-%D7%93%D7%99%D7%A0%D7%99-%D7%94%D7%A2%D7%91%D7%95%D7%93%D7%94/)
- A "service recipient" (מזמין שירות) is someone who receives services from a contractor in the course of its business, profession or public activity. — [Nevo](https://www.nevo.co.il/law_html/law00/117033.htm); [Kol Zchut](https://www.kolzchut.org.il/he/%D7%97%D7%95%D7%A7_%D7%9C%D7%94%D7%92%D7%91%D7%A8%D7%AA_%D7%94%D7%90%D7%9B%D7%99%D7%A4%D7%94_%D7%A9%D7%9C_%D7%93%D7%99%D7%A0%D7%99_%D7%94%D7%A2%D7%91%D7%95%D7%93%D7%94) (search summary)
- The law adds administrative fines of NIS 5,000–35,000 per violation per worker, alongside criminal enforcement. — [Hilan (secondary)](https://www.hilan.co.il/%D7%9E%D7%A8%D7%9B%D7%96-%D7%99%D7%93%D7%A2/%D7%91%D7%A1%D7%99%D7%A1-%D7%99%D7%93%D7%A2/%D7%9E%D7%90%D7%9E%D7%A8%D7%99%D7%9D/%D7%94%D7%97%D7%95%D7%A7-%D7%9C%D7%94%D7%92%D7%91%D7%A8%D7%AA-%D7%94%D7%90%D7%9B%D7%99%D7%A4%D7%94-%D7%A9%D7%9C-%D7%93%D7%99%D7%A0%D7%99-%D7%94%D7%A2%D7%91%D7%95%D7%93%D7%94/); [Chamber of Commerce guidance for service recipients](https://www.chamber.org.il/serviceslobby/legal/1856/2167/) (search summary). The exact section numbers for the client-liability chapter were not verified.

**Tort liability of a person who hires an independent contractor**
- Under פקודת הנזיקין סעיף 15, a person who contracts with someone who is not their employee or agent is generally not liable for that person's tort in carrying out the work. The exceptions include negligence in choosing the contractor; the full list was not extracted. — [ynet](https://www.ynet.co.il/economy/article/5451386); [mako](https://www.mako.co.il/finances-law/law-general/Article-cb5307edd41fa71026.htm) (search summaries)
- In one case a homeowner who commissioned a renovation was held 25% liable for injury to a worker. — [ynet](https://www.ynet.co.il/economy/article/5451386)

**Palestinian workers without permits (שב"ח) and foreign workers**
- Employing, housing or transporting a resident of Judea, Samaria or Gaza who is in Israel unlawfully is an offence under חוק הכניסה לישראל סעיף 12א. Secondary sources describe a 2024 amendment raising the penalty to up to 4 years' imprisonment or a fine of up to NIS 75,300, and up to four times that for a corporation. Not verified against the statute. — [Dok law firm (secondary)](https://www.dok.co.il/%D7%94%D7%A2%D7%A1%D7%A7%D7%AA-%D7%95%D7%94%D7%9C%D7%A0%D7%AA-%D7%A9%D7%95%D7%94%D7%99%D7%9D-%D7%91%D7%9C%D7%AA%D7%99-%D7%97%D7%95%D7%A7%D7%99%D7%99%D7%9D-%D7%91%D7%99%D7%A9%D7%A8%D7%90%D7%9C/); [Israel-visa (secondary)](https://www.israel-visa.co.il/%D7%94%D7%A2%D7%A1%D7%A7%D7%AA-%D7%A9%D7%91%D7%97%D7%99%D7%9D/); [ESE law firm (secondary)](https://www.ese.co.il/employment-of-the-sheba/)
- Law-firm sources say criminal liability also reaches the person who ordered the work (מזמין העבודה) where a subcontractor actually employed the illegal workers. They also say a contract clause putting the duty on the subcontractor may help in civil terms but does not protect against criminal proceedings. — [David Angel law firm: העסקת עובד זר ללא אישור באמצעות קבלן משנה (secondary)](https://www.dangel-law.co.il/%D7%94%D7%A2%D7%A1%D7%A7%D7%94-%D7%91%D7%90%D7%9E%D7%A6%D7%A2%D7%95%D7%AA-%D7%A7%D7%91%D7%9C%D7%9F-%D7%9E%D7%A9%D7%A0%D7%94-%D7%94%D7%90%D7%9D-%D7%A4%D7%95%D7%98%D7%A8%D7%AA-%D7%9E%D7%97/); [Dok (secondary)](https://www.dok.co.il/%D7%94%D7%A2%D7%A1%D7%A7%D7%AA-%D7%95%D7%94%D7%9C%D7%A0%D7%AA-%D7%A9%D7%95%D7%94%D7%99%D7%9D-%D7%91%D7%9C%D7%AA%D7%99-%D7%97%D7%95%D7%A7%D7%99%D7%99%D7%9D-%D7%91%D7%99%D7%A9%D7%A8%D7%90%D7%9C/)
- Police may close a business that employs שב"ח for up to 30 days. — [ESE: צו סגירה לעסק שמעסיק שב"חים (secondary)](https://www.ese.co.il/closure-order-for-a-business-that-employs-workers/)
- **Foreign workers without permits (חוק עובדים זרים, התשנ"א-1991):**
  - Administrative fines under תקנות העבירות המנהליות (קנס מנהלי – העסקת עובדים זרים שלא כדין), התשנ"ב-1992 are reported as about NIS 5,000–10,000 per worker, doubled for a repeat offence.
  - Criminal exposure is reported as up to 1 year or a fine of up to NIS 104,400, or NIS 52,200 where the employment was not in the course of a business.
  - All amounts are from secondary sources and not verified. — [din.co.il (secondary)](https://www.din.co.il/articles/4211/B-22748/); [Dafna Segal Fodor law firm, "2026 update" (secondary)](https://www.dsf-law.co.il/%D7%94%D7%A2%D7%A1%D7%A7%D7%AA-%D7%A2%D7%95%D7%91%D7%93-%D7%96%D7%A8-%D7%9C%D7%9C%D7%90-%D7%94%D7%99%D7%AA%D7%A8/)

### Inferences
- **Manpower licence:** a pure middleman who subcontracts whole jobs to independent businesses, and supplies no employees of his own to work under customers' direction, does not appear to fall within "קבלן כוח אדם". Gardening and softener installation are not among the licensed service-contractor fields (guarding, security, cleaning), so no licence appears to be needed. Confirm against the Second Schedule.
- **Enforcement Law:** its client-liability chapter does not appear to cover gardening or installation. Households are probably not "service recipients" in any case, because they do not receive the service in the course of a business. The middleman could be a service recipient toward its subcontractors' workers only if a covered sector were involved.
- **The real employment risk is reclassification:** a gardener economically dependent on the brand, working under its name, uniform, schedule, prices and quality control, with no independent customers, fits the integration test. Mitigations:
  - Use several subcontractors who each have their own customers, tools and registration.
  - Let them set their own schedules and price on a per-job basis.
  - Require their own עוסק file, books and insurance.
  These lower the risk but do not remove it.
- **Illegal workers:** the middleman needs a written undertaking, spot checks, and the right to terminate on the use of workers without permits. Secondary sources say none of this gives criminal immunity.

### Gaps
- The exact section of חוק הכניסה לישראל that extends liability to the person ordering work through a contractor (sources cite both 12א and 12ב2), and the current fine amounts, were not verified. Check Nevo and the Population and Immigration Authority site.
- Whether חוק עובדים זרים contains an express provision making a person who receives work through a contractor liable was not verified.
- The Second Schedule to the 1996 Manpower Law (the list of service-contractor fields) was not read in full.
- No case was found specifically on gardeners or installers reclassified as employees of a platform or brand.

## 7. Insurance the business needs

### Takeaway
No Israeli insurance is legally mandatory for a business with no employees, apart from ביטוח לאומי. Employer's liability is needed only if there are employees. The core cover for a home-services brand is third-party liability (צד ג'), plus product liability if softeners are sold, and possibly professional liability. No reliable Israeli premium figures were found. Subcontractors' insurance is normally evidenced with the regulator's mandatory uniform "אישור קיום ביטוחים" form.

### Cited Findings
- Third-party (צד ג') insurance covers bodily injury or property damage to anyone who is not an employee of the business. Its price depends mainly on the limit of liability, the type of business and claims history. A higher limit raises the premium, but not proportionally. — [Bizportal business-insurance guide](https://www.bizportal.co.il/guides/news/article/20038731)
- Third-party cover does not cover damage to adjacent property, which falls under property insurance. — [Green Invoice: ביטוח קבלנים (secondary)](https://www.greeninvoice.co.il/magazine/%D7%91%D7%99%D7%98%D7%95%D7%97-%D7%A7%D7%91%D7%9C%D7%A0%D7%99%D7%9D/)
- Employer's liability (חבות מעבידים) is not mandatory by law in Israel but is effectively required once a business has employees. It complements ביטוח לאומי and does not replace it. — [Green Invoice (secondary)](https://www.greeninvoice.co.il/magazine/%D7%91%D7%99%D7%98%D7%95%D7%97-%D7%A7%D7%91%D7%9C%D7%A0%D7%99%D7%9D/)
- There is no uniform price for small-business insurance; it depends on size, equipment and limits. — [hon.co.il (secondary)](https://www.hon.co.il/%D7%91%D7%99%D7%98%D7%95%D7%97-%D7%9C%D7%A2%D7%A1%D7%A7-%D7%9E%D7%94-%D7%94%D7%95%D7%90-%D7%9B%D7%95%D7%9C%D7%9C-%D7%95%D7%90%D7%99%D7%9A-%D7%9E%D7%A9%D7%AA%D7%A0%D7%94-%D7%9E%D7%A2%D7%A1%D7%A7/)
- Israeli insurers publish business and third-party policy wordings, but no prices were found. — [Harel third-party policy (PDF)](https://www.harel-group.co.il:443/insurance/business/policies/doclib/%d7%90%d7%97%d7%a8%d7%99%d7%95%d7%aa%20%d7%9b%d7%9c%d7%a4%d7%99%20%d7%a6%d7%93%20%d7%92/%d7%a4%d7%95%d7%9c%d7%99%d7%a1%d7%94%20%d7%9c%d7%91%d7%99%d7%98%d7%95%d7%97%20%d7%90%d7%97%d7%a8%d7%99%d7%95%d7%aa%20%d7%9b%d7%9c%d7%a4%d7%99%20%d7%a6%d7%93%20%d7%a9%d7%9c%d7%99%d7%a9%d7%99%20%d7%94%d7%a8%d7%90%d7%9c%20%d7%9e%d7%94%d7%93%d7%95%d7%a8%d7%aa%20%d7%a1%d7%a4%d7%98%d7%9e%d7%91%d7%a8%202016.pdf); [Phoenix business insurance](https://www.fnx.co.il/business-insurance/)
- The only premium benchmark found is from the US and is not applicable to Israel: a median of about $85 a month (about $1,020 a year) for general liability for remodeling contractors. — [Simply Business (US)](https://www.simplybusiness.com/?p=39929)
- The Capital Market, Insurance and Savings Authority circular 2019-1-6 sets a binding uniform form for insurance certificates (אישור קיום ביטוחים). Insurers and agents may not change its wording. The circular notes that clients commonly demand such certificates, for example for professional or employer's liability, as a condition of a service contract. — [Capital Market Authority circular 2019-1-6 (PDF)](https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/dynamiccollectorresultitem/regulation-600/he/regulation_h_2019-1-6.pdf)
- The Authority's 2026 regulation plan lists a further item on insurance certificates, so the form may change. — [Capital Market Authority regulation plan 2026 (PDF)](https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/reports/report_0015/he/regulation_plan_26_2.pdf)
- **Product liability (חוק האחריות למוצרים פגומים, התש"ם-1980):**
  - "יצרן" includes an importer who imports a foreign-made product into Israel for commercial purposes.
  - The manufacturer must compensate bodily injury caused by a product defect, with only limited statutory defences.
  - The limitation period is 3 years.
  — [Knesset law text (PDF)](https://fs.knesset.gov.il/9/law/9_lsr_210190.PDF) (search extract)
- The statute does not cover property damage claims against importers; an academic article argues for extending it. — [Mishpatim, HUJI law journal (PDF)](https://lawjournal.huji.ac.il/sites/default/files/2021-01/%D7%94%D7%98%D7%9C%D7%AA%20%D7%90%D7%97%D7%A8%D7%99%D7%95%D7%AA%20%D7%A2%D7%9C%20%D7%99%D7%91%D7%95%D7%90%D7%9F%20-%20%D7%94%D7%93%D7%A1%20%D7%A4%D7%99%D7%A8%D7%A8-%D7%90%D7%A9.pdf)

### Inferences
- **Minimum programme:**
  1. Third-party liability in the brand's name covering work at customers' homes. The policy should name the activities, cover subcontractors' acts or name them as additional insureds, and be checked for a "קבלני משנה" exclusion.
  2. Product liability, if the brand imports or private-labels water softeners. Whether a brand that only resells under its own name counts as "יצרן" was not verified.
  3. Professional or errors-and-omissions cover, optional, for design or sizing advice such as softener capacity or garden design.
  4. Employer's liability only once the business has any employee.
- **Subcontractors:** each should carry its own third-party cover, plus employer's liability if it has workers. They should provide the uniform אישור קיום ביטוחים naming the brand as an additional insured, with cross-liability, waiver of subrogation and minimum limits. NIS 1m–2m third-party limits are common in tenders but are not a sourced market norm. Section 15 of the Torts Ordinance makes negligent selection of a contractor a basis for the brand's own liability, so checking subcontractors' insurance and licences also serves as a defence.

### Gaps
- No verified Israeli premium figures (annual NIS) were found for small-business third-party, product-liability or professional-liability policies. Get quotes from two or three agents or insurers (Harel, Phoenix, Clal, Migdal, Menora) at NIS 1m and 2m limits.
- The full list of exceptions in Torts Ordinance section 15 was not extracted.
- Whether a reseller selling under its own brand is a "יצרן" under the 1980 law (the "presents itself as manufacturer" point) was not verified. Check the definition section on Nevo.

## 8. Running costs: accountant and invoicing software

### Takeaway
Indicative Israeli accountant fees are roughly NIS 300–550 a month for a single-entry עוסק מורשה and NIS 1,200–4,000 a month for a small company with double-entry books, including VAT. These ranges come from one marketplace source. Cloud invoicing software starts at about NIS 29 a month before VAT.

### Cited Findings
- **Accountant fees** (monthly, including VAT, one marketplace source):

  | Business type | Monthly fee |
  |---|---|
  | עוסק פטור | NIS 120–300 |
  | עוסק מורשה, single-entry books | NIS 300–550 |
  | Higher-volume עוסק מורשה, double-entry books | NIS 600–1,200 |
  | Small to medium company, double-entry books | NIS 1,200–4,000 |

  The annual report is often billed separately; for a עוסק פטור it is about NIS 1,000–2,000. No explicit 2026 date appears on the page. — [Midrag price guide (secondary)](https://www.midrag.co.il/Content/Price/22913)
- **Morning (Green Invoice) pricing page:**
  - Basic plan: NIS 29 a month or NIS 348 a year before VAT, up to 20 documents a month, NIS 1 per extra document.
  - Extra plan: NIS 74 a month paid annually (NIS 888) or NIS 89 a month on monthly renewal, up to 200 documents, NIS 0.50 per extra document.
  - One-month free trial.
  The page is undated. — [Green Invoice pricing](https://www.greeninvoice.co.il/pricing)
- Enforcement of the allocation-number requirements started on 1 June 2026, according to the vendor's homepage. — [Green Invoice](https://greeninvoice.co.il/)

### Inferences
- **עוסק מורשה, monthly:** about NIS 300–550 for the accountant plus about NIS 30–90 for software. Add the annual report if billed separately.
- **Company, monthly:** about NIS 1,200–4,000 for the accountant plus software. Add the NIS 1,777 Registrar annual fee and the one-off incorporation fee of about NIS 2,559.
- The principal model generates two documents per job (an expense from the subcontractor and the sale to the customer) and needs allocation-number handling above NIS 5,000. It probably pushes toward the higher software tier and toward double-entry books sooner than the agent model.

### Gaps
- iCount, Rivhit and other software prices were not found.
- Accountant fee ranges come from a single marketplace with no 2026 date; get two or three quotes.
- Bank, credit-card clearing and payment-app fees were not researched.
